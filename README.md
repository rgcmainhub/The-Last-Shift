# THE LAST SHIFT

**A horror‑survival first‑person experience built with Three.js**

### What the title means
"The Last Shift" refers to the final work‑shift of a night‑watch guard at the abandoned **Blackroot Facility**. The guard is sent down to Sublevel 3 to investigate a power failure, only to discover a dark presence that stalks the corridors. The game asks the player to survive **one last shift** and decide how to escape.

### About the game
- **Exploration & puzzles** – Find three rusted keys, power the service gate, and decide whether to seal the borehole or run.
- **Atmospheric audio** – Procedurally generated ambient sounds, footsteps, whispers and a dynamic drone that reacts to the player’s actions.
- **Procedural level** – The main level, the outdoor dead‑yard, and the underground borehole are generated each play‑through using a seeded RNG.
- **Survival mechanics** – A flashlight battery, stamina, and sanity meters. The darkness drains sanity, and the entity hunts you when you are seen or heard.
- **Multiple endings** – Escape, seal the borehole, or "join the dark" by letting the light die.

Play the game by opening the project in a browser. The `index.html` (named **TheLastShift.html**) loads the Three.js scene and handles all gameplay.
